Use your sprite in TempleOS

The package includes every frame as a .GR file and Play.HC. Extract it into a folder on your TempleOS disk, then include Play.HC. Press a key to stop playback.

CDC *dc=GRRead("Frame000.GR");
U8 *sprite=DC2Sprite(dc);
Sprite(,100,100,sprite);
Free(sprite);
DCDel(dc);

Transparent pixels keep their transparency. PNG and GIF exports use the same 16-color palette as the .GR files.
